CUSTOMER REGISTRATION PROCESS UPGRADE

A robust solution, utilizing Power Apps, Power Automate, and SharePoint, integrated directly with Salesforce, replaced the legacy system that relied on SharePoint and Nintex databases for forms, workflows, and email notifications.

The app now automates the capture of data from prospective customers in Salesforce, directing them directly to the new platform.

This completely eliminates manual data entry and accelerates cross-departmental evaluation and approval culminating in final approval by the dealership within the SAP corporate system.

In total 12 redesigned customer processes were redesigned, reducing total processing time by 55%.

55% Cycle Time Reduction
